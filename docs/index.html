Voice

An elder's own words, kept and printed as they were given — and your written voice, measured so a draft stays inside it.

Open Voice
Status Built and working offline: a scripted interview bank of 24 questions across six chapters (childhood, work, family, migrations, recipes, sayings) that asks them in order and needs no key; answers filed under their chapter; a memoir that prints the elder's words verbatim and omits empty chapters; recorded clips that play the same answer; a voice card measured from your own samples; and drafts written inside those measured bounds beside a neutral draft.
Release: Windows EXE, Android APK and Linux binary, built from this source in CI — app-latest.
Not built: speech-to-text. The Griot prompt asks for on-device transcription; there is none in this estate, so the recording is kept and played while you type or paste the words. Also not built: the LLM that would shape follow-up questions and write the memoir — the questions are a bank and the memoir is the elder's own words.

What it does

  • Asks a fixed, gentle bank of questions in order — no branching, no model, so it works with no key at all.
  • Files every answer under the chapter it belongs to, and keeps the words exactly as given.
  • Records the answer (where the browser has a recorder) and keeps the clip in memory to play alongside the words.
  • Compiles a memoir that prints only the chapters with answers, and names the ones it left out.
  • Measures your written voice — sentence length, formality, contractions, phrases — and drafts inside it, with the neutral draft beside it.

What it will not do

  • Transcribe. No speech-to-text: you type or paste the words; the recording is kept and plays the same answer.
  • Summarise the elder. The memoir prints their sentences; a summary would be the one thing this app must not do.
  • Write in a voice it has not measured. No samples, or too few words, and it refuses rather than producing a generic voice and calling it yours.
  • Drop a recording. A clip too short to hold words is kept and marked “no words heard”.
  • Use a key or a network. No fetch, no endpoint; CSP connect-src 'none'.

When things go wrong

CaseWhat happens
Bad inputA recording with no speaker named is refused — an interview with no attribution is not a memoir. A clip too short to hold words is kept and marked. A sample too short to model a voice is refused with the minimum named.
No networkNothing changes: the bank, the clips and the samples are on the device.
Storage full or unavailableThe recording stops immediately and says so, with the length it kept — a lost hour of an elder's account is not recoverable, so the failure is loud.
Corrupt saved stateReported and offered for export; a fresh session is never started over it, and audio in memory is untouched.

How to run it

  • Here: play/index.html, or install it from your browser's menu.
  • Offline file: open docs/play/index.html straight from disk.
  • Tests: node tests/self-tests.js — the spec's countable “done means” is asserted there.
