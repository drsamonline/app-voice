Voice.

The questions you will be asked, in the order they come — so you can practise the answer out loud.

nothing is recordedNo microphone, no transcription, no audio API. The app asks; you speak; nothing is captured.
01Tell us about a time you changed your mind about something that mattered.they are listening for the change, not the story
02What would you do if a deadline moved and two people disagreed?they are listening for how you decide
03Tell us about something you got wrong.they are listening for what you did next
04Why this, and why now?they are listening for a reason that is yours

The questions, in order

Each one carries a line on what is actually being listened for, because the question is rarely the thing being asked.

What it refuses

It will not record you, score you, or tell you your answer was good. Practising out loud is the whole method.
